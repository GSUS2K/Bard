**Welcome to Bard**

Link your Spotify account to check and control your own playback from Discord.
Music plays on your Spotify device; the bot does not stream Spotify audio into Discord voice.

**Get started**
Use `/link` and follow the Spotify authorization page. Spotify Premium is required for playback controls.

**Commands**
- `/nowplaying` — show the track currently playing on your Spotify account.
- `/pause` and `/resume` — pause or resume Spotify playback.
- `/skip` and `/previous` — move through your Spotify queue.
- `/playlists` — list your Spotify playlists.
- `/unlink` — remove your Spotify account from the bot.
- `/listfiles` and `/playfile` — list and play local audio files in a Discord voice channel.
- `/uploadfile` — attach an audio file in Discord and save it to the bot's music folder.
- `/stopvoice` and `/leavevoice` — stop local playback or disconnect the bot.

Commands control the Spotify account linked to your Discord account. You can continue choosing devices and controlling playback in the Spotify app.

**Owner administration**
Use `/admin status`, `/admin restart`, and `/admin update`. These commands are restricted to the bot owner ID 682288992456409096. Restart and update require a process supervisor configured to restart the bot.
